# Luna Flags

<img src="/images/2016-01/solarbodies.jpg" alt="pictures of the Moon & Earth" align="right">

The colours or sizes may change, but the idea is simple - represent the new frontier, and the old home. Where we are now (the moon) is most important, but don't forget where we were from.

### Lets keep it simple


I designed these flag options a long time ago, around the year 2001, following some discussion (and a competition) on the main [moon society](http://www.moonsociety.org/) mailing list regarding a flag. Several were produced, but they were all very complicated.

<img src="/images/2016-01/plain.jpg" alt="pictures of gray & blue circles, representing the Moon & Earth" align="left">
I'm not a graphics designer, but it was plain to me that the above images would fit perfectly. They can also be swapped around (large earth/blue circle, smaller silver moon) to signify a Earth-side installation. For comparison, I also did versions with the image of the Moon/Earth that can also be used.
